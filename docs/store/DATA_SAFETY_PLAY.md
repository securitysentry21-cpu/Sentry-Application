## 4. Why "Shared: Yes" for most rows `[LAWYER]`

Google's form asks whether data is **shared** with third parties. Its definitions exempt a service provider that processes data for the developer, a legal request, and some user-initiated transfers.

Here the data goes to the **guard's employer** (the customer security company), which the guard is told about. That is the product's purpose. The employer is not our service provider; we are the employer's service provider. So the employer is arguably a "third party" and the data is "shared".

- **Recommended (conservative):** answer **Yes, shared** for every row the employer's dashboard shows. A wrong "No" is the greater risk.
- **Alternative:** if Google or the lawyer says the employer is the data controller and we act as its service provider, the rows become "collected, not shared."
- **Crash logs** are never shown to the employer, so they are "not shared" either way.

`[LAWYER: which reading is right, and how Google treats B2B and employee-monitoring apps.]` Source of what the employer sees: `docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md` §3.2.
